Why your support matters

Running a competitive robotics team can cost over $15,000 per season. Your support ensures every student has the opportunity to learn, build, and compete.

Funds are used for:

  • Robot parts, electronics, and raw materials
  • Competition registration fees
  • Travel and logistics for events
  • Safety equipment and workshop tools
